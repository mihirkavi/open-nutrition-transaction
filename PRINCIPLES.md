# OpenNutri Principles

1. **User controlled.** Sharing requires clear authorization and can be revoked.
2. **Minimal.** Move only the fields needed for the stated nutrition purpose.
3. **Open.** Specifications and conformance tooling are publicly usable.
4. **Neutral.** No retailer, application, vendor, or data source receives preference.
5. **Extensible.** Namespaced additions can evolve without fragmenting the core.
6. **Verifiable.** Provenance, historical validity, and optional signatures are first-class.
7. **Practical.** JSON, REST, webhooks, downloads, links, QR codes, and files can coexist.
8. **Complementary.** Reuse GTIN, UPC, EAN, PLU, SKU, menu, recipe, and POS identifiers.
9. **Honest.** A purchase is not consumption; estimates are not authoritative values.
10. **Non-clinical by default.** OpenNutri exchanges nutrition data and makes no diagnosis or treatment claim.

OpenNutri does not ask food companies to open their databases. It asks the
industry to create a narrow, user-authorized interoperability layer for nutrition.
