# OpenNutri Mission

OpenNutri makes nutrition data portable, interoperable, permissioned,
trustworthy, and useful across the food and health ecosystem.

The north star is simple: every person can take their nutrition information
wherever they choose. Food businesses keep control of their infrastructure and
commercial systems; people authorize a narrow nutritional representation of
their own food events to reach a destination they select.

OpenNutri is neutral infrastructure. It is not a calorie tracker, food database,
retailer platform, medical record, advertising system, or proprietary connector.
It complements identifiers and standards already used by manufacturers,
retailers, restaurants, POS providers, health services, and researchers.

Success means a provider can implement one interface, competing applications can
consume the same trustworthy event, and the person remains in control throughout.
