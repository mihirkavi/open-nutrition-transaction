# Dietitian and Healthcare Guide

OpenNutri may reduce self-reporting friction and provide better structured dietary
context with the person's permission. It does not prove consumption, diagnose a
condition, recommend treatment, or make nutrition data inherently subject to a
particular healthcare law.

Use the least data needed, display provenance and uncertainty, separate purchase
from patient-reported consumption, define retention, honor revocation, and assess
the legal and clinical obligations of the specific implementation. Clinically
meaningful interpretation belongs above the interoperability layer.
